# HeyDart whimsical visual system

Status: implementation direction approved by Lindsay Simmons on 2026-08-07. Website release approved by Lindsay on 2026-08-08 under the authority James delegated to her. Future public deployment and Sanity publication may be approved by either James or Lindsay.

## Promise

The visual system should feel like a colorful family theme-park day—not a tropical resort, generic fantasy storybook, or glossy AI illustration.

## Palette

- Deep lagoon `#12343B`: primary dark panels and headings
- Pale aqua `#E7F5F1`: page field
- Lifted aqua `#D6EEE9`: section contrast
- Park teal `#177B72`: live-data and success accent
- Sunset coral `#D84A35`: primary action
- Coral deep `#B93425`: hover/action contrast
- Marigold `#E3A218`: decorative light and emphasis
- Bougainvillea `#B93678`: restrained punctuation
- Supporting violet `#69528F`: occasional canopy/lantern detail only

## Canonical Dart

Every Dart appearance must retain:

- orange-red fur;
- symmetrical cream muzzle, chest, belly, inner ears, and one cream tail tip;
- large amber eyes and a small black nose;
- dark bare paws and feet;
- tall pointed ears and slim upright proportions;
- exactly one slate-blue crossbody satchel with one strap, flap, and button;
- a locked personal-planner kit carried inside the satchel: the top third of a small accordion-folded pale-aqua map and one short marigold pencil. In every full-body illustration or animation, both must visibly protrude together from the opening of the existing satchel while the satchel front stays plain. The folded map uses only vague lagoon path lines and three colored dots—never a framed card, badge, label, or recognizable park layout. Keep the contents sparse and subordinate to Dart's silhouette.

No shirts, shoes, scarves, extra bags, straps, tails, limbs, duplicate foxes, or accessories beyond the locked planner kit. The planner pieces must never contain words, numbers, attraction icons, mouse-ear markers, recognizable park-map geometry, branded tickets, or copied interface elements.

## Approved park cues

- striped canopy fabric;
- lantern strings and blank pennants;
- scalloped kiosk trim;
- colorful railings;
- patterned/confetti paving;
- benches, stroller parking, families, and broad walkways;
- ordinary rounded shade trees and low shrubs.

## Prohibited visual shorthand

- palms, tropical leaves, hibiscus, dense flowers, beaches, water, jungle foliage, or resort landscaping as the default environment;
- castles, Ferris wheels, recognizable rides, attraction vehicles, copied park architecture, Disney typography/logos, mouse-ear silhouettes, costumes, or app interfaces;
- readable generated text, pseudo-text, signatures, or symbols masquerading as UI;
- beige/yellow paper washes;
- large accidental blank image fields;
- glossy fantasy/storybook rendering that erases the handmade surface.

## Production method

1. Start from the approved master image, not a prose-only fox description.
2. Generate/edit one asset at a time.
3. Reject character drift, anatomy failures, extra accessories, pseudo-text, tropical cues, or accidental IP.
4. Preserve full-frame context and inspect actual desktop/mobile crops.
5. Keep source and cropped production files separate.
6. Do not deploy website or publish Sanity assets without the applicable release approval from James or Lindsay.
